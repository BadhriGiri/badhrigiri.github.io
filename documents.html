A note on scope

Ask me for any of these and I will send it over. Two of them were never going to be published anyway: the risk register was a live file that changed weekly, and the reference notes summarise ICC standards that are not mine to republish.

Everything here was written as academic work that takes the shape of professional practice. It is careful, sourced and internally consistent, and it is not a substitute for advice from a qualified adviser.

The set

Six documents, in the order they were written

  • Risk register

    Legal risk register

    The working document the rest of the compliance set was built on. Regulatory exposure across stablecoin issuance, payment services, anti money laundering and business verification, data privacy and trade finance law, in the United Kingdom, the European Union and the United Arab Emirates. Revised continuously through the project, and reconciled in full when the licensing strategy changed in the final week.

    Working document
  • Legal instrument

    Trade Escrow Agreement

    A draft contract template for the relationship the smart contract executes. Definitions, funding, document submission, the objection window, dispute resolution through ICC DOCDEX, liability, and data protection. The custody classification is left explicitly unresolved rather than assumed, which is a flag rather than an oversight.

    Available on request
  • Risk assessment

    Legal and compliance risk assessment

    The register rewritten as a typeset, fully cited assessment with its own bibliography. Converting bullet lists and tables into cited academic prose is where most of the weak claims surfaced, because a sentence that needs a source and cannot find one is usually a sentence that was never true.

    Available on request
  • Market research

    Competitor analysis

    The live players in digital trade escrow and cross border settlement, alongside a well funded consortium platform that shut down in 2023. The failure is the more useful data point: it shows the gap is real and that being well capitalised is not sufficient to close it.

    Available on request
  • Reference note

    UCP 600, working notes

    The ICC rules that govern documentary credits, written up as a reference our document checking logic could actually be tested against. The team's shorthand was "UCP 600 inspired". That is not a standard you can check compliance against, so I wrote the standard down.

    Internal reference
  • Reference note

    Incoterms 2020, working notes

    Who bears cost and risk at each point of a shipment, and where each term transfers them. This is what determines which documents a given deal should require, so it sat directly underneath the release conditions in the product.

    Internal reference
