6 June 2026

Torquay to Exeter

Torquay railway station to the Quay Beacon in Exeter, in a single day, raising money as a group for the Devon Wildlife Trust. Fifteen of us set out. Checkpoints at Teignmouth, Dawlish, Starcross and Marsh Barton meant people could drop out and others could join partway, so eleven reached the Beacon and seven of us had walked the whole thing.

Twelve hours for 46.9 km is not a quick pace and was never meant to be. The first half along the coast was harder to navigate than it looks on a map, and the rest of the time went on talking to people and looking at the sea.

It nearly ended at Starcross. A friend called it there and took the train back to Exeter, and I was very tempted to go with her. Another friend talked me into finishing. The part nobody warns you about is that reaching the Beacon was not actually the end, because I still had 2.5 km to walk back to my accommodation. I did that on foot too.

30 May 2026

Newton St Cyres to Crediton

My first walk with the Out of Doors Society. Sixteen of us along the Creedy valley, two hours, mostly flat, no ambition beyond the afternoon.

It counts for more than the distance suggests. I spent it talking to people I had never met, and they are the ones who talked me into the Torquay walk a week later.

25 April 2026

Ivybridge

Onto the southern edge of Dartmoor and back, and my first proper moorland walk. The steepest of the three, with 351 metres of climbing inside 13 kilometres.

The pace was nobody's concern. We stopped for lunch at the top beside a small stream and stayed there a while. Five and a half hours is what happens when the walk is the excuse rather than the objective.
